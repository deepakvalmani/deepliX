Services

deepliX provides data consulting and engineering services as described in individually scoped project proposals. Each project is governed by a separate statement of work agreed by both parties.

First project — free

Your first project with deepliX is delivered at no cost. You own all deliverables immediately upon completion. There is no obligation to continue working with us.

Money-back guarantee

For all paid projects: if we do not solve the data problem defined in the statement of work, you are entitled to a full refund of all fees paid for that project. You retain ownership of all deliverables regardless of whether a refund is requested. Refund requests must be made within 30 days of project completion.

Intellectual property

All work product, including code, dashboards, documentation and processes, is owned by the client upon delivery and payment (or upon delivery for the free first project).

Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary information shared during the engagement. This obligation survives the termination of any project.

Limitation of liability

deepliX's liability for any project is limited to the fees paid for that project, or, for the free first project, to the direct cost of remedying any defect in the deliverables.

Termination

Either party may terminate a project with 14 days' written notice. Upon termination, the client receives all work completed to date.
